French Fries

Description

French fries are crispy, golden, and easy to make.

They are a simple and delicious side dish made with potatoes, oil, and salt.

Ingredients

  • 3 potatoes
  • Vegetable oil
  • Salt

Steps

  1. Peel the potatoes.
  2. Cut the potatoes into thin strips.
  3. Wash the potato strips with cold water.
  4. Dry the potatoes with a paper towel.
  5. Heat the oil in a pan.
  6. Carefully add the potatoes to the hot oil.
  7. Fry until golden and crispy.
  8. Remove the fries from the oil.
  9. Add salt.
  10. Serve hot.
