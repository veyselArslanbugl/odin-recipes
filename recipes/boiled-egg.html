Boiled egg

Description

Boiled eggs are simple, healthy, and easy to make. They are perfect for a quick breakfast.

Ingredients

  • 1 egg
  • Water
  • Salt

Steps

  1. Put the egg in a pot.
  2. Add enough water to cover the egg.
  3. Bring the water to a boil.
  4. Boil the egg for 10 minutes.
  5. Remove the egg from the pot.
  6. Let it cool for a few minutes.
  7. Peel the egg.
  8. Add salt and serve.
