Fried Egg

Description

Fried eggs are quick, simple, and easy to make. They are perfect for a simple breakfast.

Ingredients

  • 1 egg
  • 1 tablespoon of vegetable oil
  • Salt
  • Black pepper

Steps

  1. Heat the oil in a pan.
  2. Crack the egg into the pan.
  3. Cook the egg until the white is set.
  4. Add salt and black pepper.
  5. Remove the egg from the pan.
  6. Serve hot.
